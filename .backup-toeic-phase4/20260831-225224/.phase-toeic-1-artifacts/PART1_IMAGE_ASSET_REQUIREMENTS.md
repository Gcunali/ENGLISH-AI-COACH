# Part 1 Image Asset Requirements

Production assets must be original local realistic/photo-like landscape scenes, visually readable at desktop and responsive widths, with one clearly best description among four plausible statements. Essential wording, brands, copyrighted characters, ETS material, and ambiguous primary actions are prohibited.

Each image must:

- be a local PNG between 50 KiB and 10 MiB;
- have an exact SHA-256 in the Item Bank;
- use a containment-safe relative path with no symlink;
- clearly establish people/action, object state, location, or spatial relationship;
- avoid text required to solve the item;
- support one correct statement and three plausible but visually disprovable distractors.

Phase 1 includes 18 checked scenes: office filing, conference room, warehouse hand truck, outdoor restaurant, airport waiting area, construction plans, hotel reception, train boarding, road marking, store stocking, factory inspection, flower watering, chart presentation, delivery unloading, bicycle repair, kitchen preparation, car-trunk loading, and bus boarding.
