# Pedagogy

The teacher targets a 65/35 student-to-teacher speaking ratio and one to four spoken sentences. It follows the student's topic, asks one useful follow-up, avoids automatic praise, and corrects only high-value errors before returning to conversation.

Future correction modes map to frequency: Gentle handles communication-blocking errors, Balanced handles important or recurring errors, and Intensive gives more feedback without interrupting every sentence.

CEFR values are estimates supported by multiple lessons, not precise percentages. Pronunciation remains unavailable until an acoustic model evaluates audio. Vocabulary moves from New through Learning and Familiar to Mastered based on observed reuse. Periodic assessment needs enough evidence, approximately five lessons.
