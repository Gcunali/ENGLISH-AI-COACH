# A2 Curriculum Matrix

| Unit | Lesson | Communicative goal | Grammar | Vocabulary | Target expressions | Recycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Talking About the Past | Past Simple: Regular Verbs | Describe completed past actions with regular verbs and time markers. | regular past -ed and pronunciation patterns | weekend, ago, visit, arrive, leave, buy, meet, happen, regular verb | We visited the museum yesterday.; She arrived at nine o'clock.; What happened? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Talking About the Past | Common Irregular Verbs | Use frequent irregular past forms in a short account. | went, had, saw, made, took, came, and bought | visit, arrive, leave, buy, meet, happen, regular verb, irregular verb, remember | I went downtown and bought a book.; We had lunch with our friends.; What happened? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Talking About the Past | Did You...? | Ask and answer about completed past actions. | did/didn't plus base verb | leave, buy, meet, happen, regular verb, irregular verb, remember, trip, yesterday | Did you enjoy the concert?; No, I didn't stay until the end.; What happened? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Talking About the Past | Yesterday & Last Weekend | Connect several past actions to describe yesterday or a weekend. | past simple sequence with time expressions | meet, happen, regular verb, irregular verb, remember, trip, yesterday, last night, weekend | Yesterday, I worked and then cooked dinner.; Last weekend, we saw our family.; What happened? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Talking About the Past | Tell Me What Happened | Give and clarify an integrated account of a simple past event. | integrated past simple review | regular verb, irregular verb, remember, trip, yesterday, last night, weekend, ago, visit | First, I missed the bus; then I called a friend.; What did you do after that?; What happened? | Mission review |
| Stories & Experiences | What Were You Doing? | Describe an action that was in progress at a past moment. | past continuous was/were plus -ing | story, event, scene, suddenly, while, when, experience, ever, never | I was walking home at eight.; What were you doing when I called?; What happened next? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Stories & Experiences | When & While | Connect a continuing background action with a shorter past event. | past continuous with while and past simple with when | scene, suddenly, while, when, experience, ever, never, already, detail | While I was cooking, the phone rang.; I met Ava when I was traveling.; What happened next? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Stories & Experiences | Telling a Story | Organize a short story with a beginning, sequence, and ending. | past tenses with first, then, suddenly, and finally | while, when, experience, ever, never, already, detail, memory, adventure | First, we arrived at the lake.; Suddenly, it started to rain.; What happened next? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Stories & Experiences | Have You Ever...? | Ask and answer about life experience without a finished time. | present perfect with ever and never | experience, ever, never, already, detail, memory, adventure, continue, story | Have you ever traveled alone?; No, I've never traveled alone.; What happened next? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Stories & Experiences | Life Experiences | Share basic experiences and add a simple past detail. | present perfect experience plus past simple detail | never, already, detail, memory, adventure, continue, story, event, scene | I've visited Canada twice.; I went there with my family last year.; What happened next? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Stories & Experiences | A Memorable Experience | Tell a connected memorable experience using background, events, and reflection. | integrated past and present perfect review | detail, memory, adventure, continue, story, event, scene, suddenly, while | I was studying abroad when I met my best friend.; It was an experience I'll always remember.; What happened next? | Mission review |
| Plans & the Future | Going To | State planned intentions based on a decision already made. | be going to plus base verb | plan, arrangement, intention, prediction, possibility, book, pack, leave, arrive | I'm going to study tonight.; We're going to visit Boston in June.; What are your plans? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Plans & the Future | Future Arrangements | Discuss fixed personal arrangements with time and place details. | present continuous for arranged future events | intention, prediction, possibility, book, pack, leave, arrive, probably, maybe | I'm meeting Nina at six tomorrow.; We're flying on Friday morning.; What are your plans? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Plans & the Future | Will | Make an immediate decision, offer help, or state a neutral future fact. | will plus base verb and contractions | possibility, book, pack, leave, arrive, probably, maybe, forecast, decision | I'll help you carry that bag.; The meeting will start at ten.; What are your plans? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Plans & the Future | Predictions | Make and explain simple predictions about the future. | will and going to for predictions | pack, leave, arrive, probably, maybe, forecast, decision, destination, plan | I think prices will increase.; Look at those clouds; it's going to rain.; What are your plans? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Plans & the Future | Maybe, Might & Future Possibilities | Discuss uncertain future possibilities without presenting them as facts. | might/may plus base verb and maybe | arrive, probably, maybe, forecast, decision, destination, plan, arrangement, intention | We might stay an extra day.; Maybe I'll take the train instead.; What are your plans? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Plans & the Future | Planning a Trip | Agree on a trip using intentions, arrangements, decisions, and possibilities. | integrated future forms review | maybe, forecast, decision, destination, plan, arrangement, intention, prediction, possibility | We're going to visit Chicago next month.; We might book a hotel near downtown.; What are your plans? | Mission review |
| Comparing & Choosing | Bigger, Better, Faster | Compare two people, places, or products using common comparative forms. | comparatives with -er, more, and irregular better | compare, choice, option, quality, feature, price, fast, convenient, reliable | This room is bigger than that one.; The train is more comfortable than the bus.; Which option do you prefer? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Comparing & Choosing | The Best Choice | Identify an extreme or top option in a group. | superlatives with the -est, the most, and the best | option, quality, feature, price, fast, convenient, reliable, enough, too | This is the cheapest option.; That hotel has the best location.; Which option do you prefer? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Comparing & Choosing | As...As | Describe equal and unequal qualities in two options. | as adjective as and not as adjective as | feature, price, fast, convenient, reliable, enough, too, similar, different | The small model is as fast as the large one.; The bus isn't as convenient as the train.; Which option do you prefer? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Comparing & Choosing | Too Much / Not Enough | Explain why an amount or quality does not fit a need. | too much/many and not enough | fast, convenient, reliable, enough, too, similar, different, recommend, compare | This bag costs too much.; There aren't enough seats for everyone.; Which option do you prefer? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Comparing & Choosing | Comparing Options | Compare several features and give reasons for a preference. | comparative linkers and because | reliable, enough, too, similar, different, recommend, compare, choice, option | Option A is cheaper, but Option B is more reliable.; I prefer B because the battery lasts longer.; Which option do you prefer? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Comparing & Choosing | Choosing the Best Option | Reach an integrated choice by comparing needs, features, limits, and value. | integrated comparison review | too, similar, different, recommend, compare, choice, option, quality, feature | This apartment is the most convenient for us.; It's expensive, but it has enough space.; Which option do you prefer? | Mission review |
| Travel & Accommodation | At the Airport | Find a gate, understand basic departure information, and answer check-in questions. | travel questions and airport instructions | boarding pass, gate, departure, arrival, luggage, reservation, check in, available, delay | Which gate does the flight leave from?; Boarding begins at gate twelve.; Could you help me with this problem? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Travel & Accommodation | Checking In | Check in at a hotel and confirm a reservation and room details. | polite requests and reservation details | departure, arrival, luggage, reservation, check in, available, delay, canceled, receipt | I have a reservation under Rivera.; Is breakfast included with the room?; Could you help me with this problem? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Travel & Accommodation | Asking for Travel Information | Request and clarify times, platforms, locations, and prices. | indirect polite questions with could and can | luggage, reservation, check in, available, delay, canceled, receipt, single room, already | Could you tell me when the train leaves?; Do you know which platform I need?; Could you help me with this problem? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Travel & Accommodation | Travel Problems | Report a delay, missing item, or reservation problem and request action. | present/past facts with problem and request language | check in, available, delay, canceled, receipt, single room, already, yet, boarding pass | My flight has been canceled.; My luggage didn't arrive with the flight.; Could you help me with this problem? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Travel & Accommodation | Just, Already & Yet | Give concise updates about recently completed travel actions. | present perfect with just, already, and yet | delay, canceled, receipt, single room, already, yet, boarding pass, gate, departure | I've just checked in.; Has the bus arrived yet?; Could you help me with this problem? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Travel & Accommodation | Solving a Travel Problem | Explain a travel problem, clarify details, and agree on a practical solution. | integrated travel and present perfect review | receipt, single room, already, yet, boarding pass, gate, departure, arrival, luggage | My room isn't ready yet.; Could you store my luggage until two?; Could you help me with this problem? | Mission review |
