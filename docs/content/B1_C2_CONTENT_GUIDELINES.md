# B1–C2 Content Guidelines

This supplement extends `CONTENT_EDITORIAL_STANDARD_V1.md` without changing its eight-stage contract. All content is original en-US and every sixth Lesson consolidates Lessons 1–5 without introducing a major grammar topic.

| Level | Communicative demand | Theory | Vocabulary | Listening | Exercises | Guided turns |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| B1 | Explain experiences, connect reasons, and resolve familiar problems independently | 250–450 words | 10 | 5 segments | 10 | 6 / 8 / 12 |
| B2 | Structure arguments, justify choices, negotiate, and manage complex situations | 300–550 words | 11 | 6 segments | 11 | 7 / 10 / 14 |
| C1 | Sustain nuanced professional, academic, and persuasive discourse with register control | 350–600 words | 12 | 7 segments | 12 | 8 / 11 / 16 |
| C2 | Preserve subtle meaning while synthesizing, reformulating, and switching register flexibly | 350–650 words | 12 | 8 segments | 12 | 8 / 12 / 18 |

Difficulty must rise through lexical precision, natural collocation, listening density, discourse structure, abstraction, response length, stance, nuance, and register—not length alone. Repeat targets remain complete chunks accepted by the runtime. Speaking Check introduces no new language. Exercises use only the six deterministic engine types and exact-answer items only recall a finite displayed model. Guided Conversation has no pass/fail or target-usage gate. Analysis remains the empty Interactive Lesson Analysis v1 payload.
