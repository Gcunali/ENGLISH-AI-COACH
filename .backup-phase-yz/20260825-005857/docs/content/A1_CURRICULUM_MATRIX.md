# A1 Curriculum Matrix

| Unit | Lesson | Communicative goal | Grammar | Vocabulary | Target expressions | Recycling |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meeting People | Hello & Goodbye | Greet someone, respond naturally, and end a short conversation politely. | Greeting + short response; goodbye + optional good wish | hello, hi, good morning, good afternoon, good evening, goodbye, see you, nice to meet you | Hello! How are you?; Hi! I'm fine, thank you.; Nice to meet you. | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Meeting People | What's Your Name? | Ask for a person's name, give your own name, and check pronunciation politely. | What's your name? — My name is ... / I'm ... | first name, last name, full name, name, spell, letter, call, again | What's your name?; My name is Jordan.; Please call me Jo. | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Meeting People | Countries & Nationalities | Say where you are from, state your nationality, and ask another person about origin. | Subject + be + from + country; Subject + be + nationality adjective | country, nationality, language, brazil, brazilian, mexico, mexican, united states, american | Where are you from?; I'm from Brazil.; I'm Brazilian. | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Meeting People | Personal Information | Exchange basic age, city, phone, and email information in a safe everyday context. | WH question + be/do; short subject + verb answer | age, city, address, phone number, email, form, live, contact | How old are you?; I'm twenty-four years old.; Where do you live? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Meeting People | I Am, You Are, He Is | Choose the correct present form of be to identify and describe people. | I am; he/she/it is; you/we/they are | i, you, he, she, we, they, friend, neighbor | I am a new student.; You are in my class.; He is my friend. | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Meeting People | Introductions Mission | Complete a first meeting by greeting, exchanging names and origins, and closing politely. | greeting → name → question → personal detail → polite response → goodbye | introduce, meet, classmate, coworker, welcome, from, speak, contact | Hello, my name is Taylor.; Nice to meet you, Taylor.; Where are you from? | Mission review |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | Numbers 0–100 | Say, understand, and use numbers from zero to one hundred in everyday exchanges. | cardinal numbers 0–100 | number, zero, hundred, spell, phone number, email address, weekday, weekend | The total is forty-two.; My number is eighty-six.; What time works for you? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | Spelling, Phones & Emails | Spell names and exchange a practice phone number and email address clearly. | alphabet spelling and digit-by-digit numbers | hundred, spell, phone number, email address, weekday, weekend, date, o'clock | How do you spell your last name?; My practice email is maya@example.com.; What time works for you? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | Days, Months & Dates | Ask for and state weekdays, months, and simple calendar dates. | What day/date and ordinal date forms | phone number, email address, weekday, weekend, date, o'clock, noon, appointment | What day is the appointment?; It's on Monday, March fifth.; What time works for you? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | What Time Is It? | Ask for and tell common clock times. | What time, o'clock, half past, and time prepositions | weekday, weekend, date, o'clock, noon, appointment, schedule, available | What time is it?; It's half past three.; What time works for you? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | My Schedule | Describe a simple daily schedule with days and times. | present simple with time expressions | date, o'clock, noon, appointment, schedule, available, number, zero | I work at nine on weekdays.; My English class is on Tuesday.; What time works for you? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Numbers, Time & Dates | Making a Simple Appointment | Arrange a simple appointment by checking a day and time. | integrated numbers, dates, time, and availability | noon, appointment, schedule, available, number, zero, hundred, spell | Are you available on Friday?; Yes, ten o'clock works for me.; What time works for you? | Mission review |
| Family & People | My Family | Name close family members and say how they are related. | family nouns and possessive adjectives | parent, mother, father, sister, brother, child, grandparent, family | This is my sister, Elena.; We are a small family.; Who is this? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Family & People | Who Is This? | Ask and answer who a person is in a photo or group. | Who is and demonstrative this | father, sister, brother, child, grandparent, family, have, tall | Who is this in the photo?; He is my grandfather.; Who is this? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Family & People | Have & Has | Say what people have and ask about family or personal features. | have with I/you/we/they and has with he/she/it | brother, child, grandparent, family, have, tall, friendly, hair | I have two brothers.; She has brown eyes.; Who is this? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Family & People | Describing People | Give a short respectful description of a person's appearance and character. | be and have for descriptions | grandparent, family, have, tall, friendly, hair, belong, photo | My cousin is friendly and tall.; He has short black hair.; Who is this? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Family & People | Possession | Say who everyday objects and family items belong to. | possessive adjectives and apostrophe-s | have, tall, friendly, hair, belong, photo, parent, mother | This is Ana's photo.; Their house is near our house.; Who is this? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Family & People | Meet My Family | Introduce family members and combine relationship, possession, and description language. | integrated family description review | friendly, hair, belong, photo, parent, mother, father, sister | This is my mother, Grace.; She is friendly and she has curly hair.; Who is this? | Mission review |
| Daily Life | My Daily Routine | Describe the main actions in a typical day in sequence. | present simple first-person routine verbs | wake up, get dressed, breakfast, commute, work, study, lunch, exercise | I wake up at seven.; I have breakfast before work.; What do you do every day? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Daily Life | Present Simple | Use the present simple for regular actions and facts. | present simple affirmative and third-person -s | breakfast, commute, work, study, lunch, exercise, relax, always | I study English every day.; Mina works at a hospital.; What do you do every day? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Daily Life | I Don't / He Doesn't | Make clear present simple negative statements about routines. | do not and does not plus base verb | work, study, lunch, exercise, relax, always, usually, sometimes | I don't work on Sunday.; He doesn't drink coffee.; What do you do every day? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Daily Life | Do You...? / Does She...? | Ask and answer present simple questions about regular activities. | do/does questions and short answers | lunch, exercise, relax, always, usually, sometimes, never, routine | Do you exercise every day?; Yes, I do, but she doesn't.; What do you do every day? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Daily Life | Always, Usually, Sometimes | Describe how often routine activities happen. | frequency adverbs before main verbs and after be | relax, always, usually, sometimes, never, routine, wake up, get dressed | I usually eat lunch at noon.; We are sometimes busy on Friday.; What do you do every day? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Daily Life | A Day in My Life | Give an integrated short account of a normal day and ask about another person's routine. | integrated present simple and frequency review | usually, sometimes, never, routine, wake up, get dressed, breakfast, commute | I usually start work at nine.; What do you do after dinner?; What do you do every day? | Mission review |
| Home & Around Town | My Home | Name rooms and give a simple description of a home. | be, have, and room nouns | living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom, furniture, there is, there are, next to | My home has two bedrooms.; The kitchen is next to the living room.; Where is it? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Home & Around Town | There Is / There Are | Say what exists in a room or neighborhood. | there is for singular and there are for plural | bedroom, bathroom, furniture, there is, there are, next to, across from, between | There is a table by the window.; There are three chairs in the kitchen.; Where is it? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Home & Around Town | Where Is It? | Ask for and state an object's location using basic prepositions. | where questions and place prepositions | furniture, there is, there are, next to, across from, between, bank, pharmacy | Where is the key?; It's on the table next to the lamp.; Where is it? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Home & Around Town | Places in Town | Identify useful places and say where they are in town. | place nouns and there is/are review | there are, next to, across from, between, bank, pharmacy, intersection, turn | There is a pharmacy on Main Street.; The bank is across from the park.; Where is it? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Home & Around Town | Giving Directions | Give and follow short walking directions. | imperatives, turn, go straight, and location phrases | across from, between, bank, pharmacy, intersection, turn, living room, kitchen | Go straight for one block.; Turn left at the intersection.; Where is it? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Home & Around Town | Finding a Place | Ask for a destination and follow an integrated set of simple directions. | integrated town and direction review | bank, pharmacy, intersection, turn, living room, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom | Excuse me, where is the pharmacy?; Go straight and it's on your right.; Where is it? | Mission review |
| Food & Café English | Food & Drinks | Name common foods and drinks and say what you eat or drink. | food nouns and present simple | water, coffee, tea, bread, rice, fruit, vegetable, menu | I drink water with lunch.; We eat rice and vegetables.; What would you like? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Food & Café English | Countable & Uncountable | Distinguish items that can be counted from food substances and liquids. | a/an, plural nouns, and uncountable nouns | tea, bread, rice, fruit, vegetable, menu, some, any | I have an apple and two sandwiches.; We need some rice and water.; What would you like? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Food & Café English | Some & Any | Ask about and describe available food with some and any. | some in affirmatives and any in questions/negatives | rice, fruit, vegetable, menu, some, any, hungry, thirsty | There is some bread on the table.; Do we have any fruit?; What would you like? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Food & Café English | What Do You Like? | Ask about and state food preferences. | like, don't like, and preference questions | vegetable, menu, some, any, hungry, thirsty, order, check | What food do you like?; I like tea, but I don't like coffee.; What would you like? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Food & Café English | I'd Like... | Make a polite simple request for food or a drink. | would like as a fixed polite request | some, any, hungry, thirsty, order, check, water, coffee | I'd like a cup of tea, please.; Could I have some water?; What would you like? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Food & Café English | At the Café | Order food and a drink, respond to a question, and ask for the check. | integrated café-order review | hungry, thirsty, order, check, water, coffee, tea, bread | I'd like a sandwich and coffee, please.; Could I have the check, please?; What would you like? | Mission review |
| Free Time & Abilities | Hobbies & Free Time | Name hobbies and describe regular free-time activities. | like plus noun or to-infinitive | hobby, read, cook, dance, swim, play, can, free time | I like to read after dinner.; We play games on the weekend.; What do you like to do? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Free Time & Abilities | Can You...? | Ask about and state basic abilities with can. | can/can't and ability questions | cook, dance, swim, play, can, free time, right now, invite | Can you swim?; Yes, I can, but I can't dive.; What do you like to do? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Free Time & Abilities | Likes & Dislikes | Compare simple likes and dislikes and ask about preferences. | like/love/don't like plus activities | swim, play, can, free time, right now, invite, movie, park | She loves dancing.; They don't like cooking.; What do you like to do? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Free Time & Abilities | What Are You Doing? | Ask and say what is happening at the present moment. | present continuous for actions now | can, free time, right now, invite, movie, park, weekend, plan | What are you doing right now?; I'm reading a new book.; What do you like to do? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Free Time & Abilities | Let's Do Something | Make, accept, or decline a simple activity suggestion. | let's and invitation responses | right now, invite, movie, park, weekend, plan, hobby, read | Let's watch a movie tonight.; Sorry, I can't, but Saturday is good.; What do you like to do? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Free Time & Abilities | Weekend Plans | Discuss abilities, preferences, current actions, and a simple weekend plan. | integrated free-time review | movie, park, weekend, plan, hobby, read, cook, dance | Can you play tennis on Saturday?; Yes, let's meet at the park.; What do you like to do? | Mission review |
| Shopping & Everyday English | Clothes & Colors | Name basic clothing and describe its color. | be plus color and clothing nouns | shirt, pants, dress, shoes, color, size, price, cheap | This shirt is blue.; Her shoes are black.; Can you help me, please? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Shopping & Everyday English | Sizes & Prices | Ask for a clothing size and understand a simple price. | what size/how much and number review | dress, shoes, color, size, price, cheap, expensive, this | What size do you need?; How much is this jacket?; Can you help me, please? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Shopping & Everyday English | This One or That One? | Compare and choose between nearby and more distant items. | this/that and one as a substitute noun | color, size, price, cheap, expensive, this, that, try on | Do you prefer this one or that one?; I like the red one.; Can you help me, please? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Shopping & Everyday English | Can You Help Me? | Ask a store worker for an item, size, or fitting room politely. | can/could requests in a store | price, cheap, expensive, this, that, try on, receipt, broken | Can you help me find a medium?; Could I try this on?; Can you help me, please? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Shopping & Everyday English | Everyday Problems | State a simple problem and ask for practical help. | be negative, doesn't work, and help requests | expensive, this, that, try on, receipt, broken, shirt, pants | This phone charger is broken.; The card doesn't work.; Can you help me, please? | Reuses Unit vocabulary and prior beginner functions |
| Shopping & Everyday English | A1 Real-Life Mission | Complete a shopping exchange and solve one simple everyday problem. | integrated A1 shopping and help review | that, try on, receipt, broken, shirt, pants, dress, shoes | I'd like this shirt in blue, please.; It's too small; can you help me?; Can you help me, please? | Mission review |
