## Substantive CEFR-content progression

- A2 → B1: controlled connected everyday turns progress to independent explanation, fuller narrative, conditional decisions, phrasal language, and practical resolution.
- B1 → B2: familiar independence progresses to structured support, hedging, deduction, counterargument, professional negotiation, and multi-step problem handling.
- B2 → C1: structured communication progresses to abstraction, synthesis, nuanced stance, persuasion, academic/professional discourse, and register adaptation.
- C1 → C2: advanced sustained discourse progresses to fine semantic distinctions, implication, ambiguity management, meaning-preserving reformulation, rhetorical control, concision, real-time register shifts, and multi-perspective synthesis.

C2 is presented as content complexity, not native imitation or certification. Advanced content prioritizes useful collocation and natural discourse over obscure words or literary tricks.
